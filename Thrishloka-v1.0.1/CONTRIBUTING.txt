Useful work includes curve refinement, spacing and kerning improvements,
Word testing on both operating systems, additional characters and better
editable font-editor sources.

Report issues with a short sample, application and operating-system version,
font size and screenshot. State whether the issue affects TTF, WOFF2 or both.

Before proposing a change, rebuild from source and render representative
text. Keep source and binary changes consistent. Update FONTLOG.txt and
describe the change in a pull request. Preserve copyright notices and OFL
licensing for the font software. Add appropriate attribution for your work.
Only contribute material you have the right to share under the OFL.

No Reserved Font Names are declared. Modified versions must still follow
the OFL and must not imply endorsement by the original publisher.
