DESIGN HISTORY
The publisher supplied a screenshot described as a Singapore Airlines
website heading and requested a distinct font with similar neatness.
Five AI-generated visual concepts were explored. The fifth concept was
selected, narrowed during the visual-design stage, and given a lighter
Regular-weight interpretation. The original third-party screenshot is
not included in this release, and no affiliation or endorsement is claimed.

The included approved-regular-reference.png is the generated Regular
lettering specimen, not the original third-party screenshot. Separate
AI-generated uppercase and lowercase sheets extended the design.

FONT CONSTRUCTION
The script segments the supplied artwork, traces contours, fits curves,
converts them to TrueType quadratic outlines and normalizes measurements.
Several central glyphs and the numerals come directly from the approved
Regular specimen. Other letters come from the extension sheets. Punctuation
and many symbols are constructed as outlines; accents use components.
No existing third-party font binary is included as a base font.

CURRENT LIMITATIONS
Regular only; Latin-focused character set; no full professional type-design
audit; no Google Fonts approval; no native Bold or Italic. The build passes
the checks in VALIDATION.txt, but broader typography and application testing
and further outline refinement remain useful contributions.
