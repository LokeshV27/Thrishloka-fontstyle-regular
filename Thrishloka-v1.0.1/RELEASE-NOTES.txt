Thrishloka 1.0.1 — Free OFL Public Preview

Thrishloka Regular is now available free for personal and commercial use
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, credited to Sneha, India.

Includes:
- Installable TTF and WOFF2 web font.
- Uppercase and lowercase English letters, numbers, punctuation, Latin
  accents and selected symbols: 233 Unicode mappings.
- Source artwork and a rebuild script.
- Installation instructions, specimen and license.

This is an initial AI-assisted public preview. Some outlines were traced
automatically; further curve and spacing refinements are welcome. It is
not a Google Fonts-approved release. Only Regular is included.

Download the attached Thrishloka-v1.0.1-OFL.zip for the complete package.
Read README.md, INSTALL.txt and OFL.txt before redistributing.
